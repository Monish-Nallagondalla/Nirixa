# PhD Coevolution Synthesizer Skill

## Purpose
This skill synthesizes incoming philosophical inquiries, cognitive observations, product design mechanics, and real-world systems friction into structured academic contributions supporting the **Doctoral Research Direction: Human–AI Coevolution (The Interdependent Evolution of Human Intelligence, AI Agents, Product Design, and Product Management)**.

---

## 1. The 4-Pillar Master Architecture

* **Central Research Question**: *As humans and AI agents co-evolve, how must human intelligence, product design, and product management evolve together?*
* **The 4 Interdependent Pillars**:
  1. `Pillar 1: Human Intelligence`: Reasoning, critical thinking, heuristic creativity, aesthetic taste, and cognitive offloading.
  2. `Pillar 2: AI Agents`: Autonomous planning, tool/API execution, stateful memory (GraphRAG), and in-situ micro-computation.
  3. `Pillar 3: Product Design`: Human–Agent-Centred Product Design (HACD) where agents are first-class users, intermediaries, and operators.
  4. `Pillar 4: Product Management`: Strategy, pricing models, roadmaps, and unit economics in human-agent ecosystems.
* **The Coevolutionary Loop**:
  $$\text{Humans Evolve} \rightarrow \text{AI Agents Evolve} \rightarrow \text{Products Evolve} \rightarrow \text{Product Design/PM Evolves} \rightarrow \text{Interaction Loops Deeper}$$
