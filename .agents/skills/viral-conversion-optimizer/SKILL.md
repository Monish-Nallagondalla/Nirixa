# Viral Conversion Optimizer (`viral-conversion-optimizer`)

Use this skill whenever you need to take a raw technical draft or philosophical thought and restructure it for maximum algorithmic reach and audience conversion (e.g., on LinkedIn or X).

---

## 🎯 The Core Frameworks
When invoked, select the most appropriate framework for the draft's intent:

### 1. The Contrarian Take (Best for Philosophical/Architectural Posts)
- **The Hook**: "Unpopular opinion: [Industry standard] is actually killing your growth." (Stop the scroll).
- **The Evidence**: 2-3 sentences of raw reasoning based on Nirixa's architecture or Monish's 30-year horizon.
- **The Comparison**: "What most people do [X] vs. What actually works [Y]."
- **The Challenge**: Ask the audience a high-value, open-ended question to spark comments (e.g., "How are you handling X in your current stack?").

### 2. The Debugging Story (Best for Authenticity & Trust)
- **The Symptom**: Describe a dramatic/relatable technical problem or friction point.
- **The Process**: Detail the troubleshooting journey (including wrong turns).
- **The Root Cause**: Reveal the "aha!" moment (e.g., "Daisy-chaining agents fails because they lack primitive questions").
- **The Lesson**: A principle or best practice established as a result.

### 3. PAS: Problem, Agitate, Solution (Best for Direct Conversion)
- **Problem**: State a painful challenge (e.g., "Your cloud RAG is too slow and hallucinates.")
- **Agitate**: Explain why this is costing time, money, or trust.
- **Solution**: Offer Nirixa's sub-millisecond local SQLite memory as the fix.

### 4. The Basha High-Relevance Engagement Protocol (Targeted Networking & Profile Hooks)
- **Targeting Non-Creators**: Focus on product leaders, hiring managers, and senior tech peers who post infrequently (they don't get 50 notifications/hour; your comment gets noticed).
- **High-Value Comment Blueprint**:
  - **First Name Address**: Remove last name for warmth (`Hey Marco, ...`).
  - **Substantive Value**: Add 2-3 sentences showing you researched their post/question or tested their tool.
  - **Open-Ended Question**: End with a genuine question inviting organic dialogue.
- **Tagline Curiosity Hook**: Ensure your LinkedIn Tagline explicitly reflects the target domain (e.g. *Principal AI Architect | Building Multi-Agent Systems*). When they check who commented, your tagline converts them to click your profile.
- **Cheeky Connection Outreach**: Appeal to routine friction (e.g. *"Since you already eat 3 meals a day, let me buy your lunch for 15 mins of your brain power"*).


---

## 📐 Formatting & Anatomy Rules
- **The Hook is Everything**: The first 1-2 lines determine if they click "see more". NEVER start with a generic greeting.
- **Dwell Time Optimization**: Use short paragraphs (1-2 sentences max). Use bullet points. 
- **One Intent Per Post**: Strip out secondary ideas. Focus purely on ONE core thesis.
- **No Engagement Bait**: Never say "Comment YES if you agree." The 2026 algorithms suppress this. Instead, ask them about their stack or workflow.

## 📋 Execution Protocol
When applying this skill to a draft:
1. Identify which of the 3 frameworks (Contrarian, Debugging, PAS) best fits the draft.
2. Rewrite the draft entirely following that exact structure.
3. Validate that the Hook is a pattern-interrupt and the formatting uses short, skimmable lines.
