# Thought Leader Amplifier Skill (`thought-leader-amplifier`)

Use this skill whenever drafting, refining, or reviewing public posts (LinkedIn, X, blog essays) to maximize intellectual authority, organic reach, and pioneer alignment.

---

## 🎯 Core Functions

### 1. Pioneer Lineage & Tagging Recommendation
For any topic, identify the recognized pioneer who opened the research thread and suggest high-leverage citations:
- **LLM-OS & LLM-Wiki**: Cite **Andrej Karpathy** (LLM as CPU, local wiki synthesis).
- **Intelligence Primitives & ARC**: Cite **François Chollet** (Skill acquisition efficiency vs memorization).
- **World Models & Energy-Based Models**: Cite **Yann LeCun** (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture / JEPA).
- **Product Discovery & Value Risk**: Cite **Marty Cagan** (Solving primitives vs building output features).
- **Local-First & Software Craft**: Cite **Paul Graham** / **Swyx** (AI engineer ergonomics, doing things that don't scale).

### 2. The "How We Did It Better / Differently" Contrast
Always define Nirixa's distinct technical edge over existing theoretical models:
- *Theoretical Idea*: "LLMs should act as a wiki."
- *Nirixa's Working Edge*: "Built a sub-millisecond local SQLite memory core with 3-signal mathematical resonance and Socratic sparring that runs air-gapped on your laptop."

### 3. Storytelling Conversion Filter
Transforms dry technical specs into visceral narratives:
- **Hook**: The real-world friction (e.g. "We almost gave up on agent memory until we realized biological recall is associative graph traversal, not cosine distance").
- **Tension**: Why existing tools and cloud prompt-stuffing fail.
- **Proof**: Show actual code, schema, terminal output, or Telegram chat screenshot.
- **Takeaway**: Actionable first-principles conclusion.

---

## 📋 Evaluation Checklist for Every Post
- [ ] Is there an established pioneer or research paper to cite or tag for credibility?
- [ ] Does the post clearly articulate Nirixa's unique architectural differentiator?
- [ ] Is the narrative grounded in an authentic human story or real-world scar?
- [ ] Is it free of emoji clutter and sales hype?
