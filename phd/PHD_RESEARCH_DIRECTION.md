# PhD Research Direction — Human–AI–Product Coevolution

## 1. Core PhD Topic

### **Human–AI Coevolution: The Interdependent Evolution of Human Intelligence, AI Agents, Product Design, and Product Management**

### Short Description

This research explores what happens to **human thinking, cognition, intelligence, and product ecosystems when AI evolves from a tool into a continuous cognitive partner and autonomous actor**.

It investigates how sustained interaction with AI copilots and autonomous agents affects:
1. **Human Cognition**: Reasoning, critical thinking, creativity, learning, decision-making, memory, metacognition, expertise, and intellectual independence.
2. **Product Design & Product Management**: How products are conceived, architected, and managed when AI agents become **first-class users, intermediaries, decision-makers, and operators** in the product ecosystem.

The central research question is:

> **As humans and AI agents co-evolve, how must human intelligence, product design, and product management evolve together?**

# 3. The Shift in Design Paradigm: From Human-Centred to Human–Agent-Centred Design

```
Stage 1: Human-Centred Design (HCD)
└── Designed strictly for human eyes, fingers, and biological cognitive limitations (Don Norman).

Stage 2: Human + AI-Assisted Design
└── Designed for humans using AI copilots to accelerate drafting and brainstorming.

Stage 3: Human–Agent-Centred Product Design (HACD)
└── Products engineered for a dual-actor world where both biological humans and synthetic AI agents 
    discover, understand, evaluate, interact with, and act upon the product ecosystem.
```

### The Key Distinction:
> **Product design in this era isn't simply about "designing AI features into apps."  
> It is about designing products for a world where both humans and autonomous AI agents participate as active operators in the product ecosystem.**

### Core Product Design Questions:
* *How do product affordances, visual signifiers, and API contracts change when an AI agent is navigating the checkout flow, scheduling tasks, or negotiating pricing on behalf of a human?*
* *How do products balance human emotional trust with machine-readable precision?*
* *How do Product Managers define roadmaps, success metrics, and unit economics when agents perform 80% of operational interactions?*
