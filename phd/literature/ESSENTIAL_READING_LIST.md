# 📚 Essential PhD Reading List: Human–AI Coevolution & HACD

**Master Topic**: Human–AI Coevolution (Human Intelligence ↔ AI Agents ↔ Product Design ↔ Product Management)

---

## 🏛️ 1. Foundational Cognitive Psychology & Ergonomics

| Paper / Book | Authors | Year | Core Takeaway & Link to Monish's Framework |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Design of Everyday Things** | Don Norman | 1988/2013 | Knowledge in the Head vs. World, affordances, mental models, and the 3 cognitive processing layers (`OTA-046`, `OTA-035`). |
| **The Extended Mind** | Andy Clark & David Chalmers | 1998 | Active externalism: cognition does not stop at the skull; external tools become literal working memory (`OTA-040`). |
| **Thinking, Fast and Slow** | Daniel Kahneman | 2011 | Dual-process theory (System 1 intuitive heuristic vs. System 2 deliberate calculation) (`OTA-033`). |
| **Cognitive Offloading: How the Mind Uses the World** | Evan F. Risko & Sam J. Gilbert | 2016 | Explores physical and digital offloading trade-offs, metacognitive effort, and reliance thresholds (`RQ6`). |

---

## 🤖 2. Human–AI Coevolution & Cognitive Augmentation

| Paper / Journal | Authors | Venue / Year | Core Takeaway & Link to Monish's Framework |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Human–AI Coevolution: A Framework for Mutual Adaptation** | Dino Pedreschi, Luca Pappalardo, et al. | Nature / arXiv 2024 | The seminal European framework modeling the continuous feedback loop between human choices and algorithmic optimization. |
| **Shaping Human–AI Co-Evolution in Hybrid Intelligence** | Sanna Järvelä et al. (Univ of Oulu) | 2024 | Co-learning, regulated learning, and mutual adaptation in high-stakes problem-solving (`RQ7`). |
| **Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier** | Fabrizio Dell'Acqua, Edward McFowland, Ethan Mollick, et al. | Harvard Business School / 2023 | Empirical study of 758 consultants: AI boosts performance within the frontier but causes *falling asleep at the wheel* (automation bias) outside it. |
| **Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction** | Saleema Amershi, Dan Weld, Mihaela Vorvoreanu, et al. | ACM CHI 2019 | 18 foundational design heuristics for human-AI interaction across initiation, execution, and error recovery (`Pillar 3`). |

---

## 👥 3. Human–Agent Teaming, Autonomy & Agency

| Paper / Journal | Authors | Venue / Year | Core Takeaway & Link to Monish's Framework |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A Model for Types and Levels of Human Interaction with Automation** | Raja Parasuraman, Thomas B. Sheridan, Christopher D. Wickens | IEEE Trans. Systems, Man, & Cybernetics (2000) | The classical 10-level automation scale; foundation for Monish's 5-level AI Trust Scale (`OTA-039`). |
| **Designing for Human-AI Complementarity: Beyond Accuracy** | Ken Holstein, Haiyi Zhu, et al. (CMU) | ACM CHI 2023 | Moving beyond raw model benchmark accuracy to focus on team performance and error calibration. |
| **Principles of Mixed-Initiative User Interfaces** | Eric Horvitz | ACM CHI 1999 | Balancing autonomous agent action with user attention, interruption thresholds, and uncertainty. |
| **Overreliance on AI: Literature Review & Taxonomy** | Zana Buçinca, Phoebe Liu, Krzysztof Z. Gajos | ACM CSCW / IUI 2021 | Empirical methods for detecting and mitigating cognitive deskilling and uncritical acceptance. |

---

## 🎨 4. Product Design & Enterprise Work Ecosystems (HACD)

| Paper / Book | Authors | Venue / Year | Core Takeaway & Link to Monish's Framework |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Human-Centered Design of AI-Augmented Collaboration and Work** | Gerhard Schwabe, Mateusz Dolata, et al. (UZH) | UZH HISE / 2023–2025 | How enterprise collaboration and knowledge work change when AI acts as an active mediator. |
| **Responsible Artificial Intelligence: Developing and Using AI in a Responsible Way** | Virginia Dignum (Umeå University) | Springer 2019 | Socio-technical systems, agency preservation, and organizational alignment. |
| **Symbiosis: The Future of Human-AI Product Design** | Various HCI Leaders | ACM Interactions (2024) | Designing interfaces where agents are first-class users and operators (`Pillar 3 / HACD`). |
