## Executive Summary

Most enterprise AI implementations today remain trapped in the "Prompt-and-Wait Era." Product teams build tools that sit passively until a human opens a chat window and types a prompt. 

This playbook outlines the architectural shift from reactive assistants to autonomous event-driven agents. Drawing from real-world enterprise engineering friction, it details the 4 core pillars required to deploy high-trust autonomous agents in production:

1. **Event Telemetry Triggers** (Replacing human prompt boxes with automated system webhooks).
2. **Isolated Sandbox Execution** (Safely running code patches and workflows in isolated containers).
3. **Automated Evaluation Pipelines** (Passing test suites before touching production).
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Approval Gates** (Positioning human domain experts at the approval steering wheel).
