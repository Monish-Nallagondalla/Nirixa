# The North Star

## Why I Am Doing This
I am building My-OS and The Question Project for myself. 

This is not a vanity project or a content machine. This work is the compounding proof of my intellect. It is the living, evolving record of how I think, how I solve problems, and how I understand the world.

I am an AI Product Manager building systems that most people only theorize about. I have seen firsthand that the gap between "AI demos" and "AI that actually works for real humans" is enormous. I want to articulate why that gap exists in a way that changes how the world thinks about intelligence.

My goal is a TED Talk before I am 33. Before that, I want my work to be so undeniable that I receive inbound invitations from Google, NVIDIA, and Microsoft to attend and speak at their shows and exclusive talks. Not for the fame, but as concrete proof of my intellect and original thinking. 

## What I Am Afraid Of
Stagnation. 

I am competing against an unseen market of high-performers, but more importantly, I am competing against myself. I must be better today than I was yesterday. If I am not capturing my transformations and shipping my ideas, I am losing ground. 

## The Promise to Myself
On the days when I don't want to think, when I don't want to post, when the friction of building AI at EY and my startups is overwhelming—I will come back to this repository. 

This is my source of truth. It tracks my evolution. Every Original Thought Asset (OTA), every coordinate, and every question is a brick in the foundation of my career. 

The intelligence is replaceable.
The knowledge is permanent.
