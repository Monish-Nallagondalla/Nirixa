## II. Seed OTAs (Emerging Inquiries)

1. **Mixture of Thoughts**: Modeling human thinking as dynamic activation of internal cognitive experts depending on context.
2. **The First Question**: If answers generate questions, what generated the very first question in the universe?
3. **Goal Emergence**: Where do human goals originate? Can goals emerge naturally rather than being hardcoded into a loss function?
4. **The Curiosity Engine**: Can intrinsic curiosity be modeled mathematically without an extrinsic reward signal?
5. **The Computational Question**: Is a question an object, a graph, or a state transition in latent space?
6. **The Question Graph**: Storing the web of relationships between questions rather than a database of static answers.
7. **Intelligence Evolution**: Measuring how intelligence changes over a 5-year arc rather than static benchmark scores.
8. **The Memory Bridge**: Determining what psychological and mathematical forces bridge two distant memories.
9. **Originality vs. Recombination**: Investigating whether original thought is high-order recombination or true mathematical emergence.
10. **Personal Evolution Graph**: Mapping a human's evolving questions over decades as the ultimate proof of intellectual growth.
11. **The Question as Compression**: Questions as compressed representations of unknown knowledge that answers unpack.
12. **Active vs. Static Knowledge**: Knowledge that generates new questions remains alive; knowledge that sits unqueried decays.
