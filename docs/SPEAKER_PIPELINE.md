## 📜 Keynote Talk Abstract 1 (TEDx Pitch)

### Title
*Why the Future of AI Isn't Chatbots—It's Invisible Event Architecture*

### Abstract
We are currently obsessed with the "Chatbot Era"—building AI tools that sit quietly until a human opens a prompt box and types a question. But the most powerful AI implementations won't be chat windows at all. In this talk, Monish Nallagondalla breaks down the transition from reactive prompt wrappers to event-driven autonomous agents. Drawing from real-world engineering friction in high-stakes domain environments, he reveals how building autonomous sandboxes, telemetry triggers, and human approval gates is redefining the relationship between human expertise and machine intelligence.
