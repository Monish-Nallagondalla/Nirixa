# My-OS Workflow Specification

## Philosophy

My-OS should reduce thinking friction.

Information enters the system once and evolves over time.

Nothing starts as an OTA, LinkedIn post, or resume bullet.

Everything starts as an observation.

---

# Lifecycle

Observation

↓

Journal

↓

Review

↓

Knowledge

↓

Artifact

↓

Publish / Build / Archive

---

## Stage 1 — Capture

Trigger:
- New idea
- Work learning
- Conversation
- Reading
- Video
- Book
- Question

Output:

Journal Entry

Goal:

Capture quickly.

Never optimize while capturing.

---

## Stage 2 — Review

Questions:

What happened?

Why is this interesting?

Will I care in one year?

Does this connect to something else?

Can it help someone?

Outputs:

Keep

Discard

Research

---

## Stage 3 — Knowledge

Convert surviving ideas into:

Definitions

Questions

Frameworks

Research

Projects

Knowledge should be reusable.

---

## Stage 4 — Artifact

Knowledge becomes:

LinkedIn Posts

OTAs

Book Sections

Resume Evidence

Career Reflection

Experiments

---

## Stage 5 — Publish

Artifacts leave My-OS.

Examples:

LinkedIn

GitHub

Book

Presentation

TED Talk

---

## Review Cadence

Daily

Capture only.

Weekly

Review.

Monthly

Organize.

Quarterly

Reflect.

Yearly

Redesign.
