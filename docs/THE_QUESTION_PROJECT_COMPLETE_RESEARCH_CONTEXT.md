## ⚡ II. The Computational Architecture of Questions

### 3. Questions as First-Class Computational Objects
Instead of storing static answers, the knowledge graph stores questions as living nodes:
```
Question Node
    ├── Related Questions
    ├── Empirical Evidence
    ├── Counterarguments & Debates
    ├── Personal Lived Experience & Scars
    └── Active Research Threads
```

### 4. Recursive Intelligence Loops
Intelligence is not a linear pipeline; it is recursive self-inquiry:
$$\text{Question} \longrightarrow \text{Research} \longrightarrow \text{Answer} \longrightarrow \text{New Question} \longrightarrow \text{Research}$$

### 5. Memory as Graph Traversal (The Associative Bridge)
- **The Phenomenon**: *Hair $\rightarrow$ Oil $\rightarrow$ Bath $\rightarrow$ Water Heater*.
- **The Question**: How does associative recall bridge across disconnected memory nodes? Can graph traversal replicate human subconscious recall rather than high-dimensional vector cosine similarity? What determines the edge weight: *Importance, Emotion, Recency, or Intrinsic Goals*?

### 6. Intelligence $\neq$ Memory
- **Memory stores.**
- **Intelligence connects.**

## 🛡️ III. Cognitive Principles & Agent Behaviors

### 7. AI Should Debate, Not Flatter
The system must actively push back, challenge assumptions, find counter-evidence, and value productive disagreement over polite validation.

### 8. Context Over Prompts
$$\text{Repository} \longrightarrow \text{Context Loading} \longrightarrow \text{Deep Reasoning}$$
Rather than massive disposable prompts, the persistent codebase and DB serve as long-term cognitive context.

### 9. Evolution Over Optimization
The project tracks: *“How has my thinking changed over 5 years?”* rather than *“What is the quick optimal answer today?”*

### 10. Experience vs. Data
Experience changes interpretation. Two humans experience the exact same event and derive opposing conclusions. Can an AI system accumulate authentic *experience* rather than raw data?

### 11. Open Loops & Lingering Inquiries
Questions should remain active for months. The engine must periodically revisit unresolved open loops without hallucinating premature closure.

### 12. The Origin of Curiosity
Does curiosity require intrinsic uncertainty? Can computational curiosity emerge without an explicit reward function?

### 13. Intrinsic Goal Orientation
Where do human goals come from? Can questions generate goals, and can goals dissolve upon deeper inquiry?

### 14. The Compression Hypothesis
$$\text{Intelligence} \approx \text{Compression} \times \text{Prediction} \times \text{Questions}$$

### 15. Reflection as the Crucible of Knowledge
Knowledge does not emerge during the heat of experience—it crystallizes in the stillness of reflection *after* experience.

### 16. Identity Discovered Through Questioning
*Understanding who I am precedes deciding who I should become.*

### 17. Human + AI Synthesis
Not replacement or antagonism. Human intuition, lived scars, and emotional valence coupled with AI computational breadth to create superior thinkers.
