**A holistic operating model dividing personal ambition into four balanced, non-negotiable quadrants: Mission, Mastery, Money, and Mind.**

---

## Quadrant Invariants

### 1. Mission (Enterprise & Product Outputs)
* **Definition**: Real-world execution, products launched, and leadership impact.
* **Capture Protocol**: Prefix notes with `[MISSION]` or let the AI auto-classify based on project keywords.

### 2. Mastery (Deep Intellectual Inquiry)
* **Definition**: Exploration of complex technical domains, cognitive architecture, and deep reading.
* **Core Rule**: Ideas must be tested against first-principles reasoning. Passive consumption without active synthesis is rejected.
* **Capture Protocol**: Prefix with `[MASTERY]` to trigger an immediate Socratic pushback challenge.

### 3. Money (Financial Sovereignty)
* **Definition**: Capital efficiency, revenue engines, and resource allocation.
* **Core Rule**: High leverage with low ongoing maintenance overhead.
* **Capture Protocol**: Prefix with `[MONEY]` for deterministic ledger tracking.

### 4. Mind (Vitality & Cognitive Equilibrium)
* **Definition**: Physical workouts, meditation, mental clarity, and restorative rest.
* **Core Rule**: Daily non-negotiable health baseline. A degraded mind compromises all other quadrants.
* **Capture Protocol**: Prefix with `[MIND]` for daily energy check-ins.
