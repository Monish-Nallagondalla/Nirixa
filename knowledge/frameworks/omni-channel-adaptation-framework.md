# The Omni-Channel Adaptation Framework

## Philosophy
We do not create "content for X" or "content for LinkedIn". We generate **One Core Idea (OTA - One True Angle)** based on raw friction, and adapt its format to respect the culture of the distribution channel. 

## Phase 1 Rollout: X (Twitter) + LinkedIn

### Step 1: The X Hook (Testing Ground)
- **Goal**: Fast, punchy signal. Test the waters.
- **Format**: 1-3 lines. Zero fluff. 
- **Style**: Contrarian, observational, or a raw truth about AI/Product Management.
- **Output Path**: `content/x/`

### Step 2: The LinkedIn Core (Authority Layer)
- **Goal**: Professional authority, deep value, and visual real-estate.
- **Format**: Screenshot of the X Hook (or a dark-mode quote card) + 200-300 words of tactical breakdown.
- **Style**: "Here is the scar, here is why it happens, here is the architectural fix."
- **Output Path**: `content/linkedin/`

## Phase 2: Reddit (Deep Trenches) - *Pending September*
- **Goal**: Raw credibility. No self-promotion.
- **Format**: Long-form technical teardown or open question.
- **Output Path**: `content/reddit/`
