# LinkedIn Post Growth Framework & Optimization Checklist

> **Purpose**: A reference model for evaluating, drafting, and optimizing LinkedIn content to maximize scroll-stop rate, dwell time, engagement, and follower conversion.

---

## The 8 Growth Attributes for Every Post

```
Scroll-Stop (Visual / Hook) 
    ↓ 
Dwell Time (Readability / Formatting) 
    ↓ 
Value & Authority (OTA / Core Insight) 
    ↓ 
Action (Low-Friction CTA & Follower Trigger)
```

### 1. The Hook (First 140 Characters / Lines 1–2)
- **Role**: Drives the "...see more" click. 80% of post success depends on this.
- **Pattern Interrupt Options**:
  - *Counter-intuitive assertion*: "AI isn't taking your job. AI answers are."
  - *Conversation Fragment*: "I told an AI its greatest superpower was asking questions. It disagreed."
  - *High-Stakes Stance*: "The biggest mistake in AI engineering is assuming models have curiosity."

### 2. Visual Anchor (Feed Real Estate & Dwell Time)
- **Role**: Increases visual screen real estate by 300%–500% on mobile.
- **Formats (Ranked by Dwell Time)**:
  1. **PDF Carousel (Slide Deck)**: Highest dwell time. 4–7 punchy slides with bold text.
  2. **Minimalist Quote / Diagram Card**: Dark mode, branded typography, crisp visual thesis.
  3. **Real UI / Prompt Screenshot**: High authenticity (e.g. actual chat screenshot with the AI).
  4. **Text-Only**: Requires an elite 10/10 hook to stop the scroll.

### 3. Readability & Skimmability (Dwell Time Mechanics)
- **Rhythm**: 1–2 sentences per line block.
- **White Space**: High vertical breathing room.
- **Mobile First**: Optimized for single-thumb scrolling.

### 4. Core Thesis / OTA Anchor (Authority Signal)
- Must express a clear, non-generic perspective (Original Thought Asset).
- Avoid generic motivational quotes; state a sharp strategic observation derived from experience.

### 5. Low-Friction CTA (Comment Architecture)
- **High Friction (Avoid)**: "What's a deep philosophical question you carry every day?" (Requires heavy thinking → user scrolls past).
- **Low Friction (Use)**: 
  - "Do you use AI for answering or question-refining? (A or B)"
  - "Drop your favorite prompt question below 👇"

### 6. Follower Conversion Trigger (Profile CTA)
- Clearly tie the post back to your core expertise.
- *Example*: "I share the friction of building multi-agent AI systems weekly. Follow @Monish for more."

### 7. Hashtags & Metadata
- **Rule**: 2 to 3 niche, highly relevant hashtags placed at the bottom.
- **Role**: Categorization and search indexing (NOT virality).
- *Example*: `#ArtificialIntelligence #ProductManagement #FutureOfWork`

### 8. Algorithmic Distribution Rules
- **No External Links in Body**: Placing links in the post reduces reach by 50%+. Put links in comments.
- **Golden Hour Engagement**: Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes of posting to amplify feed reach.

## Case Study: Optimizing Yesterday's Post

### Original Draft
```text
I told an AI: “questions are AI’s superpower.”

It disagreed.

AI answers — brilliantly. But it doesn’t wake up wanting to know what intelligence even is. It doesn’t have a why.

I do. You do.

We’re the ones who decide what’s worth asking in the first place.

Maybe that’s the actual advantage — not that we’re smarter, but that we’re the only ones with a reason to ask.

What’s a question you’ve been carrying around that no tool would think to ask for you?
```

Our competitive advantage over AI isn't raw compute or speed. 
It's the fact that humans are the only ones with a reason to ask.
